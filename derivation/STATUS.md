# Status of the derivation

One page: what is derived, what is open, and where the evidence is.
Dates are when the result landed; the records are under `data/`, the
reasoning in [`findings.md`](findings.md), the method in
[`METHODS.md`](METHODS.md).

The driver is the crate itself, `sdr-rtl` (`src/`, its bench programs
in `examples/`). Until 2026-10-10 it was the package `sdr-rtl-derived`
in `derived/` beside an earlier driver at the crate root; that driver
was retired when this one took its place, and the records before then
name the `derived/` paths.
